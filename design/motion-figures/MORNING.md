## What changed from the v0 concepts

The figures now run 85 to 95 seconds in 9 to 11 beats, with captions you can read twice. Each one:
- sets up a concrete example;
- shows the failure happen;
- shows a second student who also works with AI and doesn't fail, with exactly one difference between the two;
- ends on the faculty check.

The testers' misreadings drove most changes:
- "Told, not shown" became "shown".
- "AI bad" became two students who both use AI.
- Students who read as lazy became sincere ones.
- Tests that faculty couldn't actually run became questions they can ask.

## Decisions waiting for you

1. **The faculty checks don't always separate the two students.** In every figure so far, both students could answer the site's verbatim check the same way. Each figure keeps the check as the headline and shows the separating move directly under it, at the same size:
   - Frame capture: "Why this frame? What would change it?"
   - Fluency substitution: "Then change one fact. What would you recommend now?"
   - Premature synthesis: "Point to one connection. What evidence would change it?"
   - Uncalibrated reliance: point to each part in turn and ask what evidence was about that part.
   - Invisible delegation: "To find out, ask each student: better by whose standard?"

   Keep the checks as they are with these follow-ups, or revise the site's checks to include them?
2. **The colour rule.** Grey is what the AI supplied, red is what a person decided, and navy is the page's ink. This refines the reel's "grey and navy are machine work", so that AI text can visibly drain into ordinary ink.
3. **Standalone pages.** Each figure works as its own page. For articles, I recommend a real path per figure (`judgmentlab.net/failure-modes/frame-capture/`) over hash routes.
4. **The book mapping.** The figures follow the book proposal's move-to-failure-mode mapping, which you haven't confirmed yet.
5. **Length.** At 85 to 95 seconds, are they the right length for teaching, or should some be cut?
6. **Figure 8's numbers.** The v0 reliance prototype showed quality changes (+17.9%, +25.1%) that are in the BCG working paper but not in the site's source spine. Add them to the spine, or leave them out?
