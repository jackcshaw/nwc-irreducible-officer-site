Judgment Lab.Motion figures: throwaway prototypes

Three prototypes for teaching the failure modes and appropriate reliance with motion. They share one runtime (motion.js) and one visual grammar: grey and navy are machine work, red is what a person decided. The proposal is in proposal.md.

    Appropriate reliance. Two look-alike tasks from the BCG study. You decide whether to rely on the AI for each, then the frontier draws itself between them, and correctness and quality move in opposite directions.
