V. Appropriate Reliance as a Teachable Competency

The educational target is specific. Students need to predict, with reasonable accuracy, when AI performs well for a given type of task and when it does not, and calibrate their use accordingly.

AI improved performance on some tasks and degraded it on others, and the boundary was not obvious in advance. Tasks that looked similar from the outside differed in whether AI helped or hurt, a finding Dell'Acqua et al. describe as the jagged technological frontier.

Your account

    Trust is a feeling that a system will perform well. Reliance is the act of accepting its output and acting on it. Appropriate reliance means accepting support when it's warranted, refusing or verifying it when it's not, and being able to give an account of the difference.

    Both answers looked ready, so the answers couldn't tell you where the line was. The tasks could. Task B turned on evidence the spreadsheet didn't show.
